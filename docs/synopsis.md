# Synopsis

Read from the script, not from a guide. Chapter ids are the script's order, not always the story's: the opening sits at the end of the dialogue block (`0x17F6D8` on).

## Who's who
- **Player** (boy or girl, named on the ship; default name set in phase 7): a 10-year-old whose parents, it turns out, took the same trials. Partner: **Hexadragon**, the "super rare" doll Dad gave as a birthday present.
- **Arisa**: rival, heir of the old Saga's heroes on DWC's side. Dad's "best friend's kid"-style secret: she was raised by DWC adults who only valued her power. She takes Tiamat's offer, is swallowed, and is saved in the end.
- **Shu, Meg, Mac, Shiron**: the anime's cast on the same ship, comic relief. Shu loses to the player in the semifinal, chases Hikaru. **Halca-sensei** shows up in disguises and takes notes.
- **Dino**: another Saga, serious; his fans call him Dino-sama.
- **DWC** (Dark Wiz Company): runs Legendz Park. **Boss** plays the Demon King. **BB, J1, J2** with **Soumu**: the anime's trio, here as shop staff and Evil Saga Commanders' help, and in the basement chase. **Ranshiin**: behind DWC, leaves when the island sinks.
- **Saga Commanders**: Ryuichiro, Reijiro, Nagisa, Kanna, Kiriko, Johnny, Andy, Hikaru. Some are "evil" by role in the Live RPG, then real.
- **Griffin, Dullahan, Thunderbird, High Elf, Glaistig, Cockatrice, Spartoi**: the island's guardians. Guard the 6 crests and the Saga's final trial.
- **Tiamat**: the dragon under the island. Bound to Arisa by contract.

## Story
1. **Ship and opening** (`0x180F1C`-`0x182188`, then `0x17F6D8`-`0x17FAD8`): birthday tickets, ship, Arisa "declares you her rival".
2. **Hidden event** (`0x17E912`-`0x17F696`): Arisa takes you to the crystal. Talispod, first Reborn, Goblin. Name your Hexadragon.
3. **Live RPG opens** (`0x17FF58`-`0x180B0C`): DWC Boss, Yeti demo, the Princess's story, the Demon King "steals" the Princess. Pass check at INFO.
4. **Stages, Dam Parts, tablets** (`0x1642B0`-`0x1747D0`): goblins and packages, sages, trials of Fire/Water/Wind/Earth, the Evil Saga Commanders, the 8 tablets (one sentence, see the glossary). Part-timers BB and J1, J2.
5. **Saga Tournament** (`0x174850`-`0x17AC36`): rounds, Hikaru steals Shu's bag, the basement lab where Legendz are made with control rings. Semifinal with Shu.
6. **Tiamat wakes** (`0x17AF9C` on): Arisa is taken. Griffin warps you to the Tower of Light. Crests of Light and Dark, snowy peak, the final trial, the camps under attack by Dark Legendz.
7. **Ending** (`0x182F5E`-`0x184898`): Element Cores, Arisa's voice tells you to break the camps' links, Tiamat falls, Arisa is saved. The island sinks, and DWC calls it all "a Park event". Hexadragon: "the ones who came before you were your parents".
