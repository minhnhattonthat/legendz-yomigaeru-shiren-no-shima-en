## How the English goes in (phase 8, `insert.graphics`)

- **Menu labels through a BG map**: maps are raw (`00 size24`, w, h, u16 entries, palette added at run time), most in the table at `0x48547C` (16 bytes each: `00080008`, w|h<<16, id, pointer). The sheet loader `0x8095710` hands out VRAM tiles in load order and keeps each base in RAM, so a sheet can grow: new tiles go on its end and a map copy points at them (`MAP_LABELS`). The title logo's loader (`0x8095684`) takes a fixed tile instead: that sheet can't grow.
- **OBJ sheets** are raw (u16 byte size, then tiles in rows); redraw in place in a copy (`BANNERS`, `RIBBON`), or clear (`BLANK_SHEETS`).
- **Finding a source**: `gfx_trace.py SCENE --at F` (VRAM dump + LZ lookup), then `gfx_cells.py` (which sheet tile is in each map cell, `--pixels` for colours), `gfx_grid.py` (zoom with the tile grid), `gfx_map.py` (render a ROM map). Sprites: read the OAM dump and search the ROM for the tiles raw.

## Text-engine screens, not graphics (listed so nobody hunts for tiles)

Same font: speaker tags (シュウ, ハルカ), dialogue, the location card, name entry's grid. Title menu (つづきから, カードモード, さいしょから, ドールしょきか): confirmed by the marker tour, strings `44128A-4412B8`, each starting with the bead its cursor sits on. Name entry needs an English grid and an English default name; a kana-only name would otherwise show as Japanese in every later box.

## Coverage: what this survey did and didn't check

- **Static sweep:** all 2,364 items rendered: 2,024 drawn on 15 pages at 1x in `work/sweep/`, 171 duplicates and 172 tiny blocks listed in `index.tsv`. Regenerate with `python -X utf8 tools/gfx_sweep.py`.
- **Read closely:** 2 full pages at 1x, plus 2x sheets of 159 blocks picked by a lettering score (`gfx_score.py`) and non-map position. At 1x, 4bpp tiles in greyscale without their palettes are hard to read, and tiles ordered for 1D sprites come out scrambled.
- **Not read closely:** the other 13 pages, including most of `0x488000-0x6FFFFF` (isometric map blocks, which only showed `EXIT`) and raw regions. Linear formats were not rendered. Treat this list as a floor.
- **Live tour:** title, title menu, ドールしょきか screen, name entry, the opening ship and first outdoor scene; phase 2 added field menu and its five commands (items through three tabs, save, おていれ, auto-adventure's intro), Talispod status, card mode's reel game, Reborn, both name entries, shop and rental screens, battle to a win and a loss, the story beats in `scenes.lua`, credits, 35 rooms. **Not reached:** random encounters, auto-adventure past its intro, the shop's sell tab (Right moved nothing with a seeded bag, so it may not exist; the arrows at the top are drawn either way). No chapter card was seen in any `story_*` run, but those runs jump past each room's own entry, so that isn't proof there are none.
- The room `LEGENDZ TOWN` / `レジェンズタウン` location card comes from the text table at `0x7EFFC4`, not tiles.

Phase 5's marker tour added G14-G16 and placed G2, G3 and G13 (75 scenes: every final frame on the half-size proof sheets, about a dozen menu and battle finals at 2x, the frame-by-frame sheets of only 6 scenes). So it's still a floor. A row can only close once a VRAM dump and `vramfind.py` confirm its source: `python -X utf8 tools/gfx_trace.py SCENE [--at FRAME]`.
